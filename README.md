# Portfolio — Red Noir Design System

A personal portfolio website built with HTML5, Tailwind CSS, and custom CSS animations, featuring a **Red Noir** aesthetic: deep black (`#0a0a0a`), vibrant crimson red (`#ef233c`), glassmorphism, glowing gradients, and subtle parallax starfield animations.

🔗 **Repository:** [https://github.com/mohamedreyhan-wq/Portfolio-Reyhan](https://github.com/mohamedreyhan-wq/Portfolio-Reyhan)

---

## 🎨 Aesthetic & Design System

- **Palette:** Deep Black (`#0a0a0a`), Dark Zinc, Vibrant Crimson Red (`#ef233c`)
- **Typography:** Inter (body) & Manrope (headings)
- **Effects:**
  - Dynamic starfield particle parallax
  - Glassmorphic backdrop-blur cards with glowing borders
  - Ambient floating crimson orbs with mouse-tracking parallax
  - Smooth scroll transitions & IntersectionObserver reveal animations
  - Red glow focus states on input fields

---

## 📐 Sections

1. **Hero Section:** Hook introducing a highly analytical and technical student with glowing crimson CTA buttons.
2. **Projects Showcase (Bento Grid):**
   - *RTA vs. Masafi Co. LLC:* Comparative business research study analyzing operational structures, hierarchies, and governance models.
   - *Bubble Tea Stall Venture:* Comprehensive financial plan, cost breakdown, break-even analysis, and investor pitch deck.
   - *Theoretical Physics Dynamics:* Structured presentation analyzing career trajectories, institutional dynamics, and academic frameworks.
3. **Skills & Academics:**
   - Coursework mastery bars for Computer Science, Accounting, Mathematics, Chemistry, and Biology.
   - Core competency breakdown covering Analytical Thinking, Research & Academic Writing, Financial Modeling, and Technical Presentations.
4. **Contact & Footer:**
   - Interactive contact form with crimson focus glow styling.
   - Social channels (GitHub, LinkedIn, Email).
